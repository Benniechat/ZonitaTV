## 1. Strategic Acquisition Objectives

Zonita TV aims to become the premier distribution and monetization partner for African digital creators, independent filmmakers, comedy troupes, and faith-based studios.

Our acquisition strategy rests on four operational pillars:
1. **Curated Quality Over Unfiltered Volume:** We do not attempt to aggregate every video on the internet. We curate compelling, culturally resonant African stories, comedy, and cinema.
2. **Mutually Beneficial Partner Models:** Provide creators with dedicated virtual channels, audience reach across Africa, performance analytics, and transparent revenue-sharing.
3. **Structured Creator Portal:** Transform manual outreach into an automated, self-serve intake workbench where creators submit metadata, verify identity, and track approval status.
4. **Indigenous African Culture Preservation:** Actively acquire and showcase films and productions across Nigerian and African indigenous languages (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, Swahili).

## 3. Commercial Deal Structures

Zonita TV supports configurable, transparent contractual frameworks logged in `distribution_agreements`:

1. **Syndication Revenue Share (AVOD):** Creator receives 50% to 70% of verified net advertising revenue generated during their scheduled broadcast windows.
2. **Fixed-Term Non-Exclusive Licensing:** Upfront licensing fee for a defined duration (6 to 24 months) across specified territories (e.g. Nigeria or Global).
3. **Digital Directory Listing & Promotion (FAST):** Non-monetary promotional syndication for emerging creators seeking audience reach, featured EPG placement, and verified audience engagement analytics.
4. **Original Co-Production Agreements:** Direct co-financing of Zonita Originals with creative partners, granting Zonita TV exclusive first-window digital broadcast rights.

## 4. Creator Portal & Inbound Intake Lifecycle

The Creator Portal (`/creator`) standardizes the partner onboarding journey:
1. **Creator Registration:** Identity verification, production company credentials, tax identification (where applicable).
2. **Content Submission:** Video upload or secure stream URL submission, synopsis, 16:9 banner artwork, 2:3 poster artwork, runtime duration, audio/subtitle language tags.
3. **Rights Warranties & Indemnity:** Electronic signature certifying 100% intellectual property ownership or authorized distribution rights.
4. **Editorial & QC Review:** Compliance review checking audio/video bitrate, content rating (PG, 15, 18), child-safety safeguards, and copyright verification.
5. **Channel Assignment & Scheduling:** Upon approval, automated ingestion into the content catalogue and scheduling queue.
